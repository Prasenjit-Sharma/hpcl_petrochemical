About HP Petrochemicals

Value addition, integrated at the source

By building cracker and derivative units into its refinery at Pachpadra, HPCL converts its own hydrocarbon streams into the polymers and chemicals that Indian industry needs — capturing more value from every barrel.

Hindustan Petroleum Corporation Limited (HPCL) is a Maharatna Central Public Sector Enterprise and a Fortune Global 500 company, majority owned through Oil and Natural Gas Corporation (ONGC).

It runs a nationwide downstream energy business — refineries at Mumbai and Visakhapatnam, joint‑venture refineries at Bathinda and Pachpadra, and a vast marketing network of retail outlets, LPG distributors, aviation stations, lubricant plants and pipelines.

Petrochemicals are a strategic growth vector: integrating cracker and derivative units with existing refineries lifts the value captured per barrel and supports India’s drive for self‑reliance in polymers and chemicals.

The complex

HPCL Rajasthan Refinery Ltd. (HRRL)

HRRL is a joint venture between Hindustan Petroleum Corporation Limited and the Government of Rajasthan, with equity participation of 74% and 26% respectively.

The project sets up a greenfield 9 MMTPA refinery‑cum‑petrochemical complex — including 2.4 MMTPA of petrochemical products — at Pachpadra, in the Balotra district of Rajasthan.

The complex carries the highest petrochemical intensity of any Indian refinery and is built for world‑scale, energy‑efficient operation with a strong local‑content and Atmanirbhar Bharat focus.

A strategically located complex

HRRL’s position gives it a range of transportation and logistics advantages.

Major transport networks.

Well connected to road and rail for efficient, cost‑effective movement — shorter transit times and lower logistics cost.

HP Durapol® — polyethylene & polypropylene

A full polymer slate on the world’s best technologies

The dual‑feed cracker yields 2.4 MMTPA of petrochemicals — polyethylene (HDPE & LLDPE), polypropylene (PPHP, ICP & RCP) and the co‑products benzene, toluene and butadiene. Two 1,000 KTA polymer trains bring HDPE, LLDPE and PP grades to converters across every segment.
