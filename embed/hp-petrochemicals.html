About HP Petrochemicals

Value addition, integrated at the source

Rather than shipping refinery streams out as low‑value fuel components, HPCL is converting them — on site — into the polymers and chemicals that Indian manufacturing still imports today.

Hindustan Petroleum Corporation Limited (HPCL) is a Maharatna Central Public Sector Enterprise and a Fortune Global 500 company, majority‑owned through Oil and Natural Gas Corporation (ONGC).

HPCL runs a nationwide downstream energy business — refineries at Mumbai and Visakhapatnam, joint‑venture refineries at Bathinda and Pachpadra, and a vast marketing network of retail outlets, LPG distributors, aviation stations, lubricant plants and cross‑country pipelines.

Petrochemicals are a strategic growth vector: by integrating cracker and derivative units with existing refineries, HPCL raises the value captured from every barrel while supporting India's push for self‑reliance in polymers and chemicals.

Complexes & operations

Where HP Petrochemicals is being built

Two integrated complexes anchor the business, supported by a dedicated polymer marketing and technical‑service organisation.

Under commissioning

Rajasthan Refinery — dual‑feed cracker & petrochemical complex

Pachpadra, Barmer, Rajasthan

A 9 MMTPA refinery integrated with a dual‑feed (naphtha + gas) cracker, designed to yield about 2.4 MMTPA of petrochemicals.

  • ~1,000 KTA polyethylene (HDPE + LLDPE swing plants)
  • ~1,000 KTA polypropylene (two gas‑phase lines)
  • BTX aromatics recovery & butadiene extraction
  • A 50:50 JV — HPCL Rajasthan Refinery Ltd (HRRL)
Development

Visakh Refinery petrochemical project

Visakhapatnam, Andhra Pradesh

A propylene‑derivative project on the modernised Visakh refinery, turning refinery‑grade propylene into polypropylene and downstream chemicals for eastern and southern markets.

  • Refinery FCC propylene as feedstock
  • Polypropylene and specialty derivatives
  • Coastal logistics for movement & export
  • Serves fast‑growing southern India demand

Applications

Materials for the everyday economy

HP Petrochemicals grades are engineered for the sectors that carry India's growth.

Packaging & FMCG

Lightweight, food‑safe films, caps, closures and rigid containers that cut material use while protecting shelf life.

Water & infrastructure

Corrosion‑free pressure pipe, drainage systems and geomembranes for irrigation, gas and city water networks.

Automotive & mobility

Impact‑copolymer compounds for bumpers, battery trays and under‑the‑hood parts that reduce vehicle weight.

Textiles & fibre

MEG and polyester intermediates plus PP staple fibre for apparel, home textiles and technical non‑wovens.

Agriculture

Mulch and greenhouse film, woven sacks and crop‑protection packaging engineered for Indian field conditions.

Consumer & healthcare

Clarified random‑copolymer for housewares and medical devices that need clarity, sterilisability and toughness.

Sustainability

Growth that respects its footprint

The complexes are engineered to high environmental standards, and the product roadmap is moving toward circular and lower‑carbon polymers.

Circularity roadmap

Design‑for‑recycling grades, mono‑material structures and participation in India's EPR framework for plastics.

Water stewardship

Zero‑liquid‑discharge philosophy, treated‑effluent reuse and rainwater harvesting — critical in a water‑scarce region.

Lower‑carbon operations

High‑efficiency furnaces, flare‑gas recovery, renewable power sourcing and a green‑hydrogen evaluation for process heat.

Community & biodiversity

Skill development, local employment and green‑belt afforestation around plant sites under HPCL's CSR programme.
