Petrochemical Complex

HPCL Rajasthan Refinery Ltd. (HRRL)

HRRL is a joint venture between Hindustan Petroleum Corporation Limited and the Government of Rajasthan, with equity participation of 74% and 26% respectively.

The project sets up a greenfield 9 MMTPA refinery‑cum‑petrochemical complex — including 2.4 MMTPA of petrochemical products — at Pachpadra, in the Balotra district of Rajasthan.

The complex carries the highest petrochemical intensity of any Indian refinery and is built for world‑scale, energy‑efficient operation with a strong local‑content and Atmanirbhar Bharat focus.

HP Durapol® — polyethylene & polypropylene

A full polymer slate on the world’s best technologies

The dual‑feed cracker yields 2.4 MMTPA of petrochemicals — polyethylene (HDPE & LLDPE), polypropylene (PPHP, ICP & RCP) and the co‑products benzene, toluene and butadiene. Two 1,000 KTA polymer trains bring HDPE, LLDPE and PP grades to converters across every segment.
